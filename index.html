Six Waters

0 of 6 waters found

Water runs through every corner of the Harvard Art Museums’ collections: drawn from wells, channeled across deserts, held in ripe fruit, and watched under moonlight. Six bodies of water are hidden in this landscape, and each one holds a work of art.
